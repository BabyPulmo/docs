# Letter of Intent — Pilot Partnership for Baby Pulmo

Date: 2026-05-25

To  
Smiling Sun Franchise Programme  
Dhaka, Bangladesh

Subject: Non-Binding Letter of Intent for Baby Pulmo Pilot Partnership

Dear Smiling Sun Franchise Programme Team,

We are writing to explore a potential pilot partnership between the Smiling Sun Franchise Programme and Baby Pulmo, an AI-assisted pediatric respiratory decision-support platform designed for low-resource and rural healthcare settings in Bangladesh.

Baby Pulmo allows caregivers to submit a child’s cough recording through WhatsApp or voice channels, after which the system provides Bangla-language guidance grounded in WHO IMCI and Bangladesh DGHS pediatric care protocols. Cases meeting severe-risk criteria may be escalated to Community Health Workers using a structured human-in-the-loop process.

Given the Smiling Sun network’s extensive maternal and child healthcare reach, we believe there may be meaningful opportunities to evaluate whether the platform can support earlier respiratory risk identification and referral workflows at the community level.

We would therefore welcome consideration for:

- A limited pilot deployment across five districts within the Smiling Sun network;
- Sharing of de-identified aggregate operational data for evaluation purposes;
- Exploration of a joint BMRC ethics and research submission related to pediatric respiratory triage.

In support of the pilot, the Baby Pulmo team would provide:

- Free platform access and technical support during the pilot period;
- Operational collaboration with participating healthcare staff where appropriate;
- Co-authorship opportunities and institutional acknowledgment in resulting research outputs or presentations.

This proposed collaboration is intended to be non-binding, non-exclusive, and exploratory in nature. All participating organizations would retain ownership of their respective systems, operational data, and intellectual property unless otherwise agreed in future formal agreements.

We would greatly value the opportunity to discuss whether Baby Pulmo may complement Smiling Sun’s ongoing maternal and child health initiatives.

If of interest, we would appreciate an indication of willingness to discuss further by 2026-06-08.
