# Letter of Intent — Pilot Collaboration for Baby Pulmo

Date: 2026-05-25

To  
Leadership, BRAC Community Health Programme  
BRAC  
Dhaka, Bangladesh

Subject: Non-Binding Letter of Intent for Baby Pulmo Pilot Collaboration

Dear BRAC Community Health Programme Leadership,

We are writing to explore a potential pilot collaboration between BRAC and Baby Pulmo, an AI-assisted pediatric respiratory decision-support platform designed for rural and low-resource settings in Bangladesh.

Baby Pulmo enables caregivers to send a child’s cough recording through WhatsApp or voice channels, after which the system provides Bangla-language guidance aligned with WHO IMCI and Bangladesh DGHS pediatric protocols. Severe-risk cases may be escalated to Community Health Workers through a human-in-the-loop workflow. The platform is intended strictly as a clinical decision-support tool and not as a standalone diagnostic device.

Given BRAC’s leadership in community healthcare delivery, we would welcome the opportunity to jointly evaluate a limited pilot deployment across five districts during Phase 1 of our implementation roadmap.

Specifically, we seek BRAC’s consideration for:

- Participation in a time-bound pilot deployment across five districts;
- Sharing de-identified aggregate operational and health outcome data for evaluation purposes;
- Exploring a joint BMRC ethics and research submission related to pediatric respiratory triage and early intervention.

In return, the Baby Pulmo team would provide:

- Full pilot access and technical support at no cost during the evaluation period;
- Training support for participating Community Health Workers where appropriate;
- Research collaboration opportunities including co-authorship and institutional acknowledgment in resulting publications, presentations, or reports.

We wish to emphasize that this proposed collaboration is non-exclusive and non-binding. All participating organizations would retain ownership of their respective data, systems, and operational processes. Any future formal partnership terms would be subject to separate mutual agreement.

If aligned with BRAC’s priorities, we would appreciate an indication of interest or willingness to discuss further by 2026-06-08.

We deeply respect BRAC’s longstanding contribution to maternal and child health in Bangladesh and would be honored to explore whether Baby Pulmo may support those efforts.
