Subject: Non-Binding Letter of Intent for Baby Pulmo Research Collaboration

Dear Research Collaboration Office,

We are reaching out to explore a potential research and pilot collaboration with icddr,b regarding Baby Pulmo, an AI-assisted pediatric respiratory decision-support platform focused on rural and underserved populations in Bangladesh.

Baby Pulmo uses pediatric cough audio recordings and AI-assisted analysis to support early respiratory risk identification using Bangla-language guidance grounded in WHO IMCI and Bangladesh DGHS pediatric protocols. Severe-risk cases are routed through a mandatory human-in-the-loop escalation workflow involving Community Health Workers.

As part of our Phase 1 roadmap, we are seeking academic and clinical collaborators to help validate the system’s real-world effectiveness, operational feasibility, and ethical deployment model.

We would therefore welcome icddr,b’s consideration for:

- Participation in a limited pilot deployment across five districts;
- Collaboration on analysis of de-identified aggregate pilot data;
- Joint BMRC ethics and research submission related to pediatric respiratory screening and triage.

The Baby Pulmo team would provide:

- Full access to the pilot system and technical implementation support at no cost during the pilot period;
- Shared research visibility through co-authorship opportunities and institutional acknowledgment in resulting publications or conference presentations;
- Open technical collaboration within a non-exclusive framework.

We wish to emphasize that this letter is non-binding and intended solely to express mutual interest in exploring collaboration. No exclusivity is requested or implied, and all participating parties would retain ownership of their respective data and intellectual property.

Given icddr,b’s global leadership in public health and implementation research, we believe your guidance could substantially strengthen the scientific rigor and ethical oversight of the project.

If aligned with your priorities, we would appreciate an indication of interest or willingness to discuss further by 2026-06-08.
